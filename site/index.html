Why Tennessee Bound

What Makes This Different

Most relocation guides give you state averages and generic advice. This one gives you county-level data and the tools to verify it yourself.

No State Averages

Tennessee property tax rates range from under $500 to over $2,500 a year on the same home value. A state average hides a $2,000 difference. Every number in these books is at the county level.

You Verify Everything

Every volume includes phone scripts and worksheets so you can call the County Trustee, the insurer, and the utility company yourself. A number you confirmed is worth more than a table we printed.

Budgets That Break

Volume 3 builds six real monthly budgets. Two of them fail. The stress test applies four shocks — insurance hike, reappraisal, medical event, car replacement — so you know where yours would break before you move.

The Freeze Nobody Mentions

Tennessee's property tax freeze can lock your bill for life. But only 27 of 95 counties have adopted it. Volume 1 maps every one, with income limits, application steps, and the mistakes that get people rejected.

A Method, Not a List

Volume 2 doesn't rank towns for you. It gives you six tests — using free public data — so you can score any county yourself in 90 minutes. The method works on any county, not just the ones we picked.

Written for 55+

Every chapter is built around retiree-specific concerns — Medicare premiums eating your COLA, aging-in-place infrastructure, nursing home quality flags, and what happens to your budget at 78 when Medigap premiums climb.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you buy.

What format are the books in?

All volumes are delivered as PDF files. You can read them on your phone, tablet, laptop, or print them out. The worksheets and scorecards are specifically designed to be printed and filled in by hand.

How do I get my books after purchase?

Instantly. After checkout, Gumroad delivers your PDF immediately — you'll get a download link on-screen and a confirmation email with the same link. No waiting, no shipping.

Do I need all three volumes?

Each volume stands on its own. Volume 1 covers taxes and the property tax freeze. Volume 2 teaches you how to score and compare counties. Volume 3 builds your actual budget and walks you through the first 90 days. If you're early in the process, start with Volume 1. If you've already picked a county, Volume 3 is where the budget math lives.

Is the information up to date?

This is the Field Edition 2026. All tax rates, income limits, Medicare premiums, and program details reflect 2026 figures. The books also include phone scripts so you can verify any number directly with your county before relying on it.

I'm not a retiree yet — is this still useful?

Yes. If you're 5-10 years from retirement and considering Tennessee, the tax freeze eligibility rules, county scoring method, and budget framework all apply. The earlier you run the numbers, the more time you have to adjust your plan.

What if my county isn't one of the 27 with a property tax freeze?

Volume 1 also covers the separate Property Tax Relief program, which is available in all 95 counties — plus the city loophole, where 36 Tennessee cities run their own freeze even in counties that don't have one. The veteran's exemption chapter has no county restriction at all.

Why do two of the six budgets in Volume 3 fail?

Because some combinations of Social Security income and Tennessee costs don't work — and it's better to see that on paper than to discover it after you've moved. The stress test chapter then shows exactly where and why the budget breaks, so you can figure out whether your numbers are closer to the ones that work or the ones that don't.

Can I get a refund?

Gumroad handles all payments and refund requests. If you're not satisfied, contact Gumroad support through your purchase receipt.
