## Upload Steps

1. Go to https://gumroad.com/dashboard
2. Click **New Product** → choose **Digital Product**
3. Paste the Name, Description, and price from above
4. Upload the PDF file for each volume
5. Set a cover image (use the flat cover PNGs from site/covers/)
6. Click **Publish**
7. Copy the share URL and paste it into `GUMROAD_LINKS` in index.html

### EUPD / GDPR Compliance on Gumroad
- Gumroad handles EU payment compliance (VAT collection, receipts)
- Enable **"Charge EU VAT"** in Gumroad Settings → Tax
- Your site's privacy policy (built into index.html) covers data handling
- Set up `privacy@tennesseebound.it.com` to receive data requests
